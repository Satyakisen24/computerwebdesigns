ESSAY OCTOBER 2026

The Silent Architecture of Margins

On the necessity of whitespace in book typography and how empty space shapes the weight of the written word.

Margins are not merely the leftovers of layout configuration; they are the buffer zone between the writer's mind and the reader's immediate reality. A page without sufficient boundary feels like a crowded room, forcing words to scramble for attention. When we afford a text its natural margins, we give thoughts room to resonate, expand, and finally settle...

POETRY AUGUST 2026

Echoes on Plastered Walls

A brief observation of shifting morning light patterns against rough-cast studio brickwork.

The light cuts thin at half-past seven long white bars across the floor dust motes drifting like minor planets settling slow beside the door. No sound but the radiator's hum and plaster cooling in the seam we sit and watch the shadows come to wake us from a quiet dream.
